          <h3 class="theory-panel__heading">1. Reported Speech (Câu tường thuật)</h3>
          <p><strong>Statements:</strong> She said (that) + S + V (lùi thì)</p>
          <p>Ví dụ: "I like cats." → She said she liked cats.</p>
          <p><strong>Questions:</strong> He asked + question word + S + V (lùi thì, không đảo ngữ)</p>
          <p>Ví dụ: "Where do you live?" → He asked where I lived.</p>
          <p><strong>Commands:</strong> She told + person + to + V</p>
          <p>Ví dụ: "Be quiet." → She told us to be quiet.</p>
          <p><strong>Requests:</strong> He asked + person + to + V</p>
          <p>Ví dụ: "Can you help me?" → He asked me to help him.</p>
        </div>

        <div class="theory-panel__section">
          <h3 class="theory-panel__heading">2. Reflexive Pronouns (Đại từ phản thân)</h3>
          <p>myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, yourselves, themselves</p>
          <p>Ví dụ: I did it <strong>myself</strong>. / They enjoyed <strong>themselves</strong>.</p>
        </div>

        <div class="theory-panel__section">
          <h3 class="theory-panel__heading">3. Wish (Câu ước)</h3>
          <p>S + wish(es) + S + V (past simple / could + V)</p>
          <p>Ví dụ: I wish I <strong>could swim</strong>. / She wishes she <strong>had</strong> more time.</p>
        </div>

        <div class="theory-panel__section">
          <h3 class="theory-panel__heading">4. Question Tags (Câu hỏi đuôi)</h3>
          <p>Câu khẳng định → tag phủ định, và ngược lại.</p>
          <p>Ví dụ: You're tired, <strong>aren't you</strong>? / He can't swim, <strong>can he</strong>?</p>
