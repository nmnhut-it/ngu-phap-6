        <div class="theory-panel__section">
          <h3 class="theory-panel__heading">4 THAT — Thay Cho WHO / WHICH (Nhưng KHÔNG Thay WHERE!) </h3>
          <p><strong>Quy tắc:</strong></p>
          <ul>
            <li> <strong>THAT</strong> thay được <strong>WHO</strong> (cho người): <em>The boy <strong>that</strong> got lost = The boy <strong>who</strong> got lost</em></li>

            <li> <strong>THAT KHÔNG</strong> thay được <strong>WHERE</strong>: <em>The park <strong>where</strong> we play ≠ The park <strong>that</strong> we play</em></li>
          </ul>

            <strong>Ví dụ:</strong><br>

            This is the park <strong>that</strong> we play. (SAI! Phải dùng WHERE)
          </div>
        </div>
